# DigitalBurj Academy design
Mode: Operate, with readable mentor explanations inside the mission workspace.

Brand source: shamhar07-max/DigitalBurjFinalMain, exact assets from its current repository snapshot. The academy workspace uses the repository's warm ivory ground (#f6f5f1), white work surfaces, deep green ink (#0f1714), orange-red primary accent (#f23a1d), darker accessible action red (#c9260e), and muted green-gray copy (#5d6560). The repository's Plus Jakarta Sans carries headings and Instrument Sans carries interface and body text. Fonts are self-hosted. Existing logos contain opaque off-white backgrounds and are displayed on matching light surfaces, preserving source pixels.

Signature interaction: a mentor introduces the current step; the learner opens an explanation, predicts a result, runs a separate live demonstration, and returns to their own editable work. Mentor demonstrations never change assessment evidence or complete a stage.

Layout: restrained working dashboard; readable progress rail, focused work surface, short instructor paragraphs and clear next action. No decorative hero, game points or pretend conversational AI. Actual computed traces, results and failures are the teaching material. Each of the 12 stages has a plain-language title beside its canonical code. Layout becomes a horizontal stage rail and one work column on mobile. Content is visible by default; brief state transitions respect reduced motion.
