# Corrected Academy workflow

30 September 2026. The user clarified that the intended 3D feature is an animated receipt printing after payment. The manufacturing course, STL generator, geometry controls, cost worksheet and their entry points have been removed. Eighteen guided courses, 180 project recipes, eleven integrated tools and the original 48 programmes remain.

## Learning journey

1. Choose a pathway and make it the current learning goal.
2. Confirm access. The free foundation and existing access continue directly to learning. Paid access follows reviewed terms and secure checkout.
3. Learn and practice. Each project advances through understand, inspect, make, check, explain and package. Future stages require the preceding evidence; tool drafts remain beside the lesson.
4. Export evidence and submit for qualified assessment. Review feedback and independent verification remain separate from local practice checks.
5. Request the pathway certificate only after all required projects are independently verified.

The home page and grouped navigation reflect this order. Removed pathway selections fall back to choosing a new pathway. Prior local evidence remains in backups rather than becoming inaccessible through a rendering error.

## Purchase and receipt flow

Checkout snapshots the product title, price, currency and access scope. Success and cancellation return to that specific order. The page reads the buyer's private order status and waits for confirmation; URL parameters never authorize payment or access.

A matched, signed provider event records the payment receipt and purchased entitlements in one database batch. Replays preserve the same receipt. The receipt-printing animation then plays, with the confirmed amount, reference, timestamp and next-pathway action. Replay, reduced-motion support, printing/PDF and a JSON record are available.

Partial refunds update the cumulative refunded amount while preserving the original payment record. Full refunds and disputes withdraw the purchase entitlement. Out-of-order smaller refunds do not reduce the recorded refund total or reactivate the order.

The private practice deployment provides a clearly labelled animation sample under Access & receipts. It creates no payment, order, entitlement or credential. Live identity and merchant services remain unconfigured.
